Our Journey

Building Excellence Since 2013

From our foundation in Salem to delivering Government and Private infrastructure projects across Tamil Nadu, our journey reflects continuous growth, quality, and commitment.

2013

KSP Construction was established in Salem with a vision to deliver reliable civil engineering and infrastructure solutions.

2015

Expanded our capabilities in road construction, BT & CC roads, drainage works, and public infrastructure development.

2018

Successfully executed major Government projects for State Highways, PWD, Railways, and Corporation departments across Tamil Nadu.

2021

Strengthened our operations with modern construction machinery, asphalt plant facilities, and an experienced technical workforce.

2024

Continued delivering high-quality Government and Private infrastructure projects with a strong focus on safety, quality, and timely execution.

2026

Proudly serving Tamil Nadu with over 13 years of experience, where 90% of our projects are Government infrastructure works and 10% are Private construction projects.

About KSP Construction

Leading Civil Engineering & Infrastructure Firm

Since our inception in 2013, KSP Construction has stood as a beacon of engineering excellence in Salem and throughout Tamil Nadu. Executing large-scale public infrastructure and private civil projects demands absolute precision, regulatory compliance, and high-tier equipment. Whether delivering state highways, PWD structures, or corporate facilities, our focus remains on durability and safety.

To maintain our track record of on-time delivery, we leverage in-house asphalt plants, high-capacity road rollers, and modern earth-moving machinery. We take pride in building infrastructure that empowers communities and powers economic growth across the region.
